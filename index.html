Pick log Every pick, why it was made and how it finished · click a row for the reasons

How it's picked: each update ranks every bet on today's board that hasn't tipped off, including player props, spreads, totals and moneylines. Every candidate must be backed by recent results (60%+ hit rate over the last 10 for props, spreads and totals; a winning record for moneylines), have a sane edge (props 6–40% of the line, spreads 1.5–6 pts, totals 3–12 pts, moneylines 4–20 points of win probability over the price), and a price no worse than -160 (-250 for moneylines). Props also need a healthy player with steady minutes. Score = 55% recent hit rate + 45% edge size. The pick locks at tip-off and is saved permanently in data/potd_log.json with its reasons and result.

Each rung is an alternate line at DraftKings. Book % is the chance the price implies; Model % blends the model's projection with the player's last 15 games. Rungs where Model % beats Book % are value (positive EV). The suggested ladder spreads one stake across the best value rungs, for example 50% on the safest rung, 30% on the middle and 20% on the long shot, so one hit covers part of the misses and a big night pays a lot.
