Pick log Every pick, why it was made and how it finished · click a row for the reasons

How it's picked: each update ranks every bet on today's board that hasn't tipped off, including player props, spreads, totals and moneylines. Every candidate must be backed by recent results (60%+ hit rate over the last 10 for props, spreads and totals; a winning record for moneylines), have a sane edge (props 6–40% of the line, spreads 1.5–6 pts, totals 3–12 pts, moneylines 4–20 points of win probability over the price), and a price no worse than -160 (-250 for moneylines). Props also need a healthy player with steady minutes. Score = 55% recent hit rate + 45% edge size. The pick locks at tip-off and is saved permanently in data/potd_log.json with its reasons and result.

How alt-line ladders work (tap to hide)
1
Pick one player and stat. DraftKings sells the same stat at many lines: 8+ rebounds pays little, 17+ rebounds pays a lot. Each line is a rung.
2
Split one stake across 2-3 rungs. A safe rung he usually clears, a middle rung, and a long shot. They are separate bets, not a parlay, so each rung pays on its own.
3
The safe rung protects you. If he has a normal night, the safe rung gets most of your stake back. If he has a big night, every rung hits and the long shot pays several times your stake.
4
Only rungs with value. We only suggest rungs where our chance is higher than the book's (positive EV). The "What happens" table on each card shows exactly what you win or lose at every result.
Reading a rung: Book says is the chance the odds imply · We say is our chance (our projection, pulled halfway toward DraftKings' main line to stay honest, blended with his recent games) · Edge is ours minus the book's · Recent is how often he cleared it lately · EV is the average return per bet over the long run.
Example stake split 50% / 30% / 20% across the suggested rungs

Each rung is an alternate line at DraftKings. Book says = the chance the price implies; We say = the model's projection blended with the player's recent games. Rungs where we beat the book have value (positive EV). The suggested ladder splits one stake across the best value rungs (50% safe, 30% middle, 20% long shot; 60/40 with two rungs), so a normal night gets most of the stake back and a big night pays a lot. Payouts shown are at DraftKings' price and round to the cent. For entertainment only; win chances are estimates.
